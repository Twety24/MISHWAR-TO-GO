✳GOOD QUESTION

Before you make a move.

The quick answers to how Mashaweer is meant to work.

Do I get to set the price?+

You suggest a price and, in the full product concept, compare driver offers before choosing. The final price depends on the request and offers.

Can I move furniture too?+

That’s part of the idea. You’d select a vehicle that suits the size and type of your load, from small parcels to larger items.

Can I book a driver on this website?+

Not yet. This is a front-end concept demo. It doesn’t connect to real drivers, bookings, or payments.
